Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Equilynx Private Limited ("Equilynx", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit equilynx.in or engage with our deep-tech research and enterprise IT consulting services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you fill out our contact form or apply for a role. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of your message or inquiry.

We also automatically collect limited technical information — such as browser type, device information, and aggregated usage analytics — to understand how visitors interact with our website and to keep it secure and performant.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries, requests, and applications.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services and website.
  • To communicate with you about engagements, collaborations, or opportunities you have expressed interest in.
  • To monitor and protect the security and integrity of our website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and to measure website traffic and performance. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit, and you can control cookies through your browser settings. Declining cookies may affect certain non-essential features but will not prevent you from browsing the site.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and process inquiries — for example, our form-handling provider (Formspree) and our content delivery and analytics provider (Cloudflare). These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at contact@equilynx.in or visit our contact page.
