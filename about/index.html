About Equilynx

Equilynx Private Limited is a Hyderabad-based deep-tech company operating two divisions — a Quantum, AI & Post-Quantum Cryptography research startup, and an Enterprise IT Consulting practice — united by the same engineering rigor.

Equilynx Private Limited was incorporated in 2025 as a deep-tech startup specializing in Quantum Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Post-Quantum Cryptography, directly aligned with India's National Quantum Mission objectives for critical infrastructure protection and sovereign technological capability.

Alongside our research division, our Enterprise IT Consulting practice brings that same engineering rigor to Indian businesses navigating digital transformation, cloud adoption, and rising cybersecurity risk — including our own post-quantum cryptography middleware.

We bridge the gap between open-ended research and production-grade engineering: from NIST/FIPS-aligned cryptographic primitives and applied AI systems to full-lifecycle enterprise delivery and managed support.
