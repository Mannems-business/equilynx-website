Equilynx is a DPIIT-recognized startup specializing in Quantum Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and Post-Quantum Cryptography. Incorporated in 2025, we directly align with India's National Quantum Mission objectives for critical infrastructure protection and sovereign technological capability.

We bridge the gap between open-ended research and production-grade engineering, architecting NIST/FIPS-aligned cryptographic primitives, applied AI systems, and the exploratory groundwork for quantum-era computing.

Our first research line to reach commercial maturity — post-quantum cryptographic middleware — now protects enterprise networks against Harvest-Now, Decrypt-Later threats, and represents the kind of translation from lab to deployed product that defines how we work.
