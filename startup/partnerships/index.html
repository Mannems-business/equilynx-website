National Quantum Mission Alignment

India's National Quantum Mission establishes explicit mandates for securing critical communication infrastructures through crypto-agility and localized data resilience. Equilynx is built to directly serve the objectives of the Quantum Communication vertical, positioning our research group to interface with sandboxes such as the IITM CDOT Samgnya Technology Foundation (Quantum Communication Hub) as those programs mature.
