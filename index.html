Equilynx Private Limited is a registered startup under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, India, incorporated in September 2025. Our core focus is research and experimental development on natural sciences and engineering.

We bridge the gap between quantum-inspired thinking and practical, real-world solutions — acquiring, developing and maintaining the technology, infrastructure and talent required to push boundaries in science and engineering.

From forming strategic collaborations with institutions and government bodies, to securing intellectual property rights and DPIIT Startup India recognition — we are building the foundation for next-generation innovation from India to the world.
