National Quantum Mission (NQM) Alignment

India's National Quantum Mission establishes explicit mandates for securing critical communication infrastructures through crypto-agility and localized data resilience. Equilynx is built to directly serve the objectives of the Quantum Communication vertical, positioning our research group to interface seamlessly with sandboxes such as the IITM CDOT Samgnya Technology Foundation (Quantum Communication Hub). Our mission is to accelerate sovereign self-reliance (Atmanirbhar Bharat) by turning frontier research into deployable, quantum-resistant capability.

Research Pillars

Quantum Computing

Algorithm design and simulation work exploring near-term quantum advantage, with a focus on cryptographically relevant problems and hybrid classical-quantum pipelines.

Artificial Intelligence

Applied machine learning for anomaly detection, adversarial modeling, and decision systems that hold up under real-world enterprise load.

Post-Quantum Cryptography

NIST/FIPS-aligned lattice-based primitives (ML-KEM, ML-DSA) engineered in Rust — our first research line to reach production maturity as a deployable middleware product.

Emerging Technologies & Distributed Systems

Exploratory work on formal verification, high-throughput distributed protocols, and the infrastructure needed to run quantum-safe systems at enterprise scale.
